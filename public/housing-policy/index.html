January 1, 2027 is the deadline

Your board needs the policy language before the deadline gets here.

HUD's Notice PIH 2026-15, issued May 14, 2026, sets the compliance date: the full HOTMA income and asset rules apply to every HUD-50058 with an effective date of January 1, 2027 or later. Authorities that start annual reexaminations 120 days early will need to begin using HOTMA policies … beginning in September 2026.

Two groups sit outside that date. The notice excludes Moving to Work agencies and authorities that currently use HUD's Family Reporting Software exclusively. If your authority is one of them, the January 1 compliance date does not apply to you.

But January 1 is not really the working deadline.

Your updated policies still have to be reviewed, placed on the agenda, and adopted. If your board meets monthly, you may only have a handful of meetings left.

Sonder handles the document work so your staff can focus on getting the policy through your own review and adoption process.

The gap check

Start with a $500 HOTMA gap check.

You do not have to commit to a rewrite first.

Send your current ACOP or Administrative Plan and Sonder will review it against the HOTMA provisions that affect your policies.

One document, twenty-seven items, built only from HUD's own material: the March 14, 2024 list of discretionary HOTMA policies, HUD Exchange's Sample Admin and ACOP HOTMA Policies, and Notice PIH 2023-27 Revision 3 (April 16, 2026) with its attachments. Each item cites the HUD page it comes from.

You receive a page-by-page report showing (see a sample gap check):

Present
The provision is already addressed at the current figure or requirement.
Outdated
Your document addresses it, but still contains an older figure, definition, or rule.
Missing
A required policy provision or decision could not be found.
Unclear
The subject appears in the document, but the policy does not clearly make the required decision.
Not addressed
An optional policy choice is not currently included.

Every finding includes:

  • the page in your document
  • the language currently there
  • what needs attention
  • the supporting HUD source

The figures used are the CY2027 inflation-adjusted values: $109,797 asset limitation, $54,898 asset threshold, $525 dependent deduction, $575 elderly or disabled deduction, 0.38% passbook rate.

So you are not getting a generic HOTMA checklist. You are getting a map of exactly what needs attention in your own policy.
