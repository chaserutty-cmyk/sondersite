Your policies were due in 2024. Full HOTMA compliance is due in 2027.

HUD Notice H 2024-04 required covered multifamily owners to update their TSP and EIV policies and procedures by May 31, 2024, and make the revised TSP publicly available.

Notice H 2025-07 subsequently extended the deadline for full HOTMA compliance to certifications effective January 1, 2027 or later.

These are separate requirements. Owners must have their policies updated, but must also follow HUD's implementation instructions before applying the new HOTMA rules to tenant certifications.

A revised TSP is not just an internal document. It is part of what HUD's contract administrators review during Management and Occupancy Reviews (MORs).

Updated your policies in 2024? They may still need attention.

HUD has revised its implementation guidance since the original policy-update deadline.

In April 2026, Notice H 2026-05 and Revision 3 of Notice H 2023-10 changed the interim reexamination requirements when household members are added or removed, including a limited exception tied to an owner's written policy.

HUD has also published the inflation-adjusted figures effective January 1, 2027:

  • $109,797 net family asset limitation for applicable Section 8 PBRA and Section 202/8 programs
  • $54,898 threshold used for asset self-certification, imputed asset income, and non-necessary personal property
  • $525 dependent deduction
  • $575 elderly or disabled family deduction
  • 0.38% passbook savings rate

These amounts apply according to the property's program requirements and HOTMA implementation status. The asset limitation does not apply to every HUD multifamily program.

If your policies already reference HUD's annually adjusted amounts, those provisions may not need rewriting. If they contain older fixed figures, outdated requirements, or unresolved owner choices, Sonder identifies the specific sections that need attention.

The goal is not to rewrite a plan that already works. It is to find and correct what HOTMA actually requires.

Service scope

This service is intended for properties covered by HUD's multifamily HOTMA implementation guidance, including Section 8 Project-Based Rental Assistance and other applicable HUD multifamily programs. Requirements are assessed by program. Properties participating only in LIHTC, or receiving assistance through PHA-administered Project-Based Vouchers, may require a different policy review.

Work is limited to applicable HOTMA policy updates. It is not a comprehensive review of every federal, state, or local occupancy requirement.

Not included: HUD model lease revisions, tenant certifications, HUD-50059 processing, TRACS or software configuration, EIV system access, or legal review.

Client documents are used for the engagement and are not published as samples without permission.
